1) Entities and Attributes (with type) :

MUSICIAN :
MusicianID (Primary Key)
Name (Simple)
Address : Composite attribute (Street + City)
Phone (Simple)

INSTRUMENT :
Name (Primary Key unique)
MusicalKey (Simple)

ALBUM :
AlbumID (Primary Key)
Title (Simple)
CopyrightDate (Simple)

SONG :
Title (Primary Key unique)
Author (Simple)
##################################################################################################

2) Relationships and Their Cardinality :
Plays : 
MUSICIAN ↔ INSTRUMENT
M:N
A musician may play several instruments, and an instrument may be played by several musicians

Contains :
ALBUM → SONG
1:M
An album has several songs, but each song belongs to only one album

Performs :
MUSICIAN ↔ SONG
M:N
A song is performed by one or more musicians, and a musician performs several songs

Produces
MUSICIAN → ALBUM
1:M
A musician (as producer) may produce several albums, but each album has exactly one producer
##################################################################################################

3) Mapping the ERD to Tables :

MUSICIAN
(MusicianID PK, Name, Street, City, Phone)
Address (composite) is split into two separate columns: Street and City.

INSTRUMENT
(Name PK, MusicalKey)

ALBUM
(AlbumID PK, Title, CopyrightDate, ProducerID FK → MUSICIAN.MusicianID)
The FK goes here because the Produces relationship is 1:M and ALBUM is on the "Many" side.

SONG
(Title PK, Author, AlbumID FK → ALBUM.AlbumID)
The FK goes here because the Contains relationship is 1:M and SONG is on the "Many" side.

Musician_Instrument 
(junction table for the M:N relationship)
MusicianID FK → MUSICIAN
InstrumentName FK → INSTRUMENT
Primary Key = (MusicianID, InstrumentName)

Musician_Song 
(junction table for the M:N relationship)
MusicianID FK → MUSICIAN
SongTitle FK → SONG
Primary Key = (MusicianID, SongTitle)
